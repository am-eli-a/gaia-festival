Our merch

Our merchandising is much more than a souvenir. It is 100% eco-friendly and supports inclusive employment thanks to our partnerships with social integration workshops.
We offer a sustainable and ethical collection, mainly made in France using recycled materials and organic cotton:
• Urban Apparel: Discover our T-shirts, hoodies, and accessories such as bucket hats and caps.
• Zero-Waste Accessories: Get equipped for the event and everyday life with our reusable water bottles and mugs, as well as our practical tote bags.
Every purchase supports our Zero Trace commitment !

Practical informations

How to get to the festival

Avoid traffic jams and parking queues: choose the Zero Carbon Bus and our partner shuttles. Thanks to our partnerships with SNCF and the local TADAO network, you benefit from discounted fares for an eco-friendly and stress-free journey. If you are coming from Belgium or Switzerland, dedicated coach lines and train packages are available to ensure a smooth and easy arrival.

Camping

To make your stay as comfortable as it is unforgettable, we offer two accommodation options. Outdoor Camping provides the classic festival atmosphere on a secure, supervised site. For those who prefer peace and guaranteed shelter, an Indoor Accommodation Area is also available.

About us

Our story, our commitment


The GAÏA FESTIVAL was born from a simple belief: it is possible to combine a passion for music with absolute respect for the planet. The adventure began in 2025 when three friends, all passionate about music, realized they couldn’t find a truly ecological festival.
So we decided to create the event we dreamed of attending: the first festival with a certified Zero Carbon Logistics system.
Our mission goes beyond concerts. We are committed to the Social and Solidarity Economy (SSE). By prioritizing inclusive employment and eco-friendly materials, we guarantee an ethical and accessible festival for everyone.
Come and experience three days of Pop, Rap, and Rock music in harmony with the environment.
